01 · The problem

A normal IT website would not work for us

The usual Odoo partner site

  • Long text about modules and "digital transformation"
  • Stock photos of people at laptops
  • Looks the same as every other partner
  • Asks visitors to trust claims they can't see

What technext.asia does

  • Shows Odoo working: apps, data and steps moving live
  • Lets visitors click and try, on almost every page
  • Has its own face, Nexi, that nobody else has
  • Shows proof by demo, while case studies are still coming

02 · The choices

Six design choices, and why each one

Moving hero scenes

Four rotating scenes: Odoo apps orbiting one database, a lead-to-cash map, the industries, and Nexi Explains.

Why: the visitor gets "one system for everything" in 5 seconds, no reading.

Nexi, the mascot

A friendly 3D robot who guides, explains and appears in the videos, posts and ads.

Why: a technical product needs a human face. People remember a character, and ad viewers recognise her on the site.

Interactive demos

45 small working demos: stock rotation, payroll, process maps, chatbots, network sims.

Why: trying beats reading. It is the proof while we build case studies.

Industry worlds

Construction, F&B, retail, medical, manufacturing, eCommerce, travel, health and wellness. Each has its own scene.

Why: an owner wants to see their own business, not a generic ERP.

Light, blue, flat Odoo icons

White background, TechNext blue, the official flat Odoo app icons, no heavy dark sections.

Why: it reads as calm and trustworthy, and sits next to Odoo's own look.

One booking path

Every page ends in the same 30-minute discovery call, booked straight into Odoo.

Why: all the design has one job: get the call booked.

03 · Heavy, but not slow

Rich design, kept smooth and accessible

Heavy design only works if it still feels fast. These are built in.

About half the GPU load

Hero animations went from ~40% GPU to ~20% after the performance pass, with no animation removed.

Lighter on phones

Phones get a lite Nexi, only on her slides, and she pauses when out of view.

Calm mode

Visitors who turn on reduced motion get still versions (93 reduced-motion rules).

Search engines see text

Google and audit tools get the first slide with the main heading, no animation.

06 · Honest trade-offs

What it costs us, and how we handle it

More to maintain

A Python build generates the pages from one design system, so a change is made once.

A 4-second intro

Good for first impressions, but speed tools count it. Shorten it if scores matter.

Still missing

Real case studies and the Meta Pixel. Both are next on the list.
